## Rules

For every release-distributed asset, record:

- asset path/name;
- asset type;
- source/provenance;
- creator/provider;
- licence/right basis;
- date obtained;
- permitted commercial use;
- attribution/notice obligation;
- restrictions on logos/people/endorsement;
- final reviewer;
- evidence location.

## Event/customer media

Photographs, videos, receipts, WhatsApp exports and documents supplied by a customer or photographer may remain the property of their creator/customer and may carry personal-information implications.

Before release:

- confirm the customer's right to upload/use the material;
- restrict access by business and authorised roles;
- keep private media behind authenticated/authorised delivery;
- document any licence/consent terms needed for publication or reuse;
- do not use customer/event media for unrelated marketing without a separate basis and approval.
