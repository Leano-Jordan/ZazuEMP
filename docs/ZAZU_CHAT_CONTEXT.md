## Product identity

- Product: **Zazu — Event Management Platform** (not catering software).
- Repository: `Leano-Jordan/ZazuEMP`.
- Canonical branch: `main` only; never create or update other branches.
- The event is the central operational record. Catering, equipment hire, sound/AV, décor, rentals, baking and photography are example configurable service capabilities, not product boundaries.
