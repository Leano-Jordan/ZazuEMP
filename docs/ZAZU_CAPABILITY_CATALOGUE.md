# Zazu EMP - Capability Catalogue Direction

This is the reusable capability vocabulary for the Work requirement and quoting layers. It is not a hard-coded list of services that every business must offer.

## Supported examples

### Catering and food
- Wedding catering
- Funeral catering
- Baking and cakes
- Cookies and dessert service

### Event hire and rentals
- Tent hire
- Chair hire
- Table hire
- Furniture hire
- Crockery/cutlery hire
- General equipment hire
- **Camera hire**

### Event services
- Sound hire
- DJ service
- Decor service
- Event coordination
- **Photography / Photographer**
- Videography

### Other event-service patterns
- Transport
- Staffing
- Venue-related services
- Custom packages
- Combined service packages

## Product rule

A capability belongs in the reusable business catalogue when it describes something that the business can repeatedly provide, hire, sell or package.

The actual Work record should carry the specific quantity, specification, timing and commercial context.

Examples:

- Capability: Photography
- Work requirement: Wedding photography, 1 service, full-day, ceremony + reception
- Quote line: the actual priced commercial offer for that work

and:

- Capability: Camera hire
- Work requirement: 2 cameras, 1 day, specific model/specification
- Quote line: the actual priced commercial offer for that hire

Do not force photography, camera hire or any other capability into every business account. Businesses should define their own catalogue.
