## Director position

Zazu EMP is **not brand/trade-mark cleared for commercial launch**.

This record separates preliminary evidence from an official CIPC search, professional clearance opinion, or trade-mark registration.

## Current evidence

CIPC's official IP Online service provides a free basic trade-mark search and states that search results are valid as at the search date but do not grant rights or constitute a trade-mark application. CIPC also offers more detailed search services.

A current CIPC-published journal contains a South African **ZAZU LIFE (PTY) LTD** mark in Class 36 for financial, monetary and banking services. This is evidence of an existing South African ZAZU mark, although the goods/services differ from Zazu EMP's event-management software use.

The repository's earlier commercial audit also recorded public South African use of the Zazu name in finance and events/catering contexts. Those observations are not determinations of infringement.

## Required before commercial launch

1. Perform the official CIPC word-mark search for **ZAZU**.
2. Search relevant Nice classes for the actual Zazu EMP software/services and intended commercial activities.
3. Review confusingly similar marks, not only exact matches.
4. Obtain professional trade-mark clearance if commercial investment proceeds.
5. Decide whether to retain **Zazu / Zazu EMP** or rename before launch.
6. If retaining the name, preserve the search report and clearance decision as release evidence.
7. Do not represent the name as registered or cleared before the above is completed.
