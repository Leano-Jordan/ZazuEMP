## 1. Service

Zazu is provided as an event/business-management application. The exact modules and deployment model are those described in the applicable order, proposal or product documentation.

## 2. Account and access

The customer is responsible for:

- maintaining accurate account information;
- protecting credentials;
- limiting access to authorised personnel;
- reporting suspected credential or security compromise promptly;
- using roles and permissions appropriately.

Zazu should not promise uninterrupted availability where the deployment is local/offline or otherwise dependent on customer hardware/network conditions.

## 3. Customer data

The customer remains responsible for determining what information it enters into Zazu and for having the appropriate rights/permissions to use that information.

Customer content may include customer records, event details, documents, receipts, photographs and other business material.

The provider's rights in the Zazu software do not transfer ownership of customer content.

## 4. Acceptable use

The customer must not intentionally use Zazu to:

- gain unauthorised access to another business's records;
- upload unlawful or malicious material;
- bypass authentication/authorization controls;
- interfere with service integrity;
- misuse personal information.

## 5. Backups and recovery

The final contract must clearly state:

- whether backups are included;
- who performs them;
- where they are stored;
- the customer's responsibilities for local installations;
- any recovery limitations.

A backup feature must not be described as an absolute guarantee of lossless recovery.

## 6. Offline/local deployment

Where Zazu is deployed locally, network access may be unavailable while core operations remain available to the extent supported by the installed version.

The product must not be described as having full disconnected multi-device sync unless that capability is actually implemented and verified.

## 7. Payments and financial records

Unless explicitly integrated and stated otherwise, Zazu records business payment information entered by authorised users. It should not be represented as processing customer funds through an external payment gateway unless that integration exists.

## 8. Intellectual property

The provider retains ownership of the Zazu software and original provider-owned materials, subject to third-party licences and any customer-owned content.

Third-party assets/components remain subject to their own terms.

## 9. Privacy

The final agreement must incorporate or reference the applicable privacy notice and data-processing/operator arrangements.

## 10. Support

Support scope, response targets, maintenance windows and contact channels must be completed from the actual commercial offering.

## 11. Changes / termination

Final legal terms must address service changes, termination, customer-data return/export, deletion and retained records.

## 12. Liability and other legal terms

Final legal counsel should add the provisions appropriate to the actual jurisdiction and sales model, including limitations, warranties, dispute handling, notices and other mandatory consumer/commercial requirements where applicable.
