## Official South African clearance path

CIPC provides:

- a basic, free trade-mark e-search described as a cursory/preliminary search;
- a special preliminary trade-mark search using TM2, listed by CIPC at R190 per class;
- IP Online search/application facilities for more detailed trade-mark work.

Official entry points:

- CIPC IP / trade marks: https://www.cipc.co.za/?page_id=1423

## Director preliminary market scan — 2026-10-04

### Finding BR-01 — direct South African software/business-name collision

Public evidence shows **Zazu South Africa / Zazu SA** has been used for a South African SME business-finance platform covering areas such as business finance, accounts, invoicing and expense management.

A public Zazu site identifies Zazu SA (Pty) Ltd and a South African company registration. Public material also indicates a later rebrand toward **Manza**, but legacy Zazu public pages/app presence remain observable.

**Assessment:** HIGH clearance relevance.

Why it matters: this is not merely a distant consumer brand. It overlaps software, business administration and commercial services in South Africa.

This is not a conclusion that Zazu EMP cannot use the name. It means official trade-mark clearance is materially necessary.

### Finding BR-02 — event-sector collision outside South Africa

**Zazu Events** is publicly used by an event-management/catering business in Australia.

**Assessment:** MEDIUM global-name relevance, lower immediate South African risk unless expansion is planned.

### Findings interpretation

A public-name collision is not equivalent to a registered trade mark, and a rebrand announcement is not equivalent to legal clearance.

Do not mark the Zazu name "cleared" until the relevant official register searches and, where appropriate, qualified trade-mark review are complete.

## Search scope for the official clearance

The final search should cover, at minimum:

- `ZAZU`
- `ZAZU EMP`
- `ZAZU EVENT` / other directly descriptive combinations;
- materially similar spellings and phonetic variants;
- the classes relevant to the actual commercial offering.

The correct classes and scope should be confirmed by a trade-mark professional based on the real software/service model. Do not assume one class covers every future use.

## Commercial protection actions

Before committing to substantial brand spend:

1. Complete the CIPC searches and preserve the result/evidence.
2. Assess relevant prior marks, owners, classes and status.
3. Obtain professional clearance where the collision is commercially material.
4. Decide whether to register `Zazu`, `Zazu EMP`, the logo, or a broader portfolio.
5. Preserve dated evidence of first brand use and original brand creation.
6. Keep a fallback product-name exploration alive until clearance is satisfactory.
