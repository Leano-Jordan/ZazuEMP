## 1. Who provides Zazu

Zazu EMP is currently developed and operated by Isaac Junior Lehlogonolo Maluleka as an individual/solo operator. Rosscore Labs is a future business identity and must not be described as an incorporated legal entity until registration is complete.

## 2. What Zazu is

Zazu EMP is an event and catering/business-management application. Depending on the enabled release features, it may manage business accounts, customers, events/jobs, services, purchasing, inventory/assets, documents, invoices, payment records, reports and related operational records.

## 3. Information that may be processed

Depending on the deployment and features used:
- account identity, email address and authentication/session metadata;
- business/workspace details;
- customer and contact names, telephone numbers and email addresses;
- event/job details, notes and operational records;
- documents, receipts, photographs and other attachments uploaded by authorised users;
- profile and business-branding images;
- invoice, quote and payment records entered for business administration;
- audit, security and support records needed to operate and protect the service;
- technical request/error information generated by the application.

Zazu should not be described as collecting information that the actual deployed feature set does not collect.

## 4. Purposes

Information may be used to provide event and business-management functionality; maintain account, workspace and role-based access; connect customer and job records to business operations; generate invoices, reports and operational records; secure the service, diagnose faults and investigate incidents; provide requested support; maintain backup and recovery capability; and meet applicable legal, regulatory and contractual obligations.

New or secondary purposes must be assessed and disclosed before introduction.

## 5. Roles and responsibility

### Customer-managed information

For personal information a customer enters about its own clients, the customer will ordinarily determine why and how that information is processed and is expected to act as the responsible party under POPIA where applicable.

### Hosted deployment

For hosted customer workspaces, the Zazu provider will ordinarily process customer personal information on the customer's documented instructions and should be governed by an appropriate written operator agreement where POPIA applies.

The provider may separately process limited account, subscription, security and billing information for its own service administration and legal obligations. The final notice and contract must describe those purposes accurately.

### Local/offline deployment

For local installations, the customer ordinarily controls the local device, database, network and backups. The provider does not receive routine access to local customer records merely because the software is installed. If the customer requests support that requires access to personal information, access must be authorised, limited to the support purpose and ended when no longer needed.

## 6. Data ownership and minimisation

Customer content remains the customer's content. The software licence does not transfer ownership of customer records, documents, photographs or business information to the provider.

Customers should enter only information needed for a legitimate business purpose and should ensure they have the rights and permissions required to use uploaded material.

## 7. Storage and third parties

Hosted deployments are intended to use South African infrastructure where commercially and technically suitable. The current hosting shortlist is xneelo Cloud in Johannesburg; no provider is represented as engaged until an account and service are actually provisioned.

The final notice must identify actual hosting, email, backup, payment, analytics and support providers that process personal information, their role, processing location and any cross-border transfer where applicable. No provider should be listed merely because the application could integrate with it.

Local deployments store data in the customer's environment, subject to the installed configuration.

## 8. Security

Zazu uses technical controls including authentication, business-context access controls, role/permission checks, private media delivery and audit/security records. These controls reduce risk but do not guarantee that every security incident can be prevented.

Customers must protect their credentials, devices, local networks and backups. The provider must restrict support access and protect hosted infrastructure.

## 9. Retention

Retention depends on purpose and applicable law. Tax and company recordkeeping periods do not automatically apply to every personal-information category. The provisional policy is recorded in RELEASE_RETENTION_DISPOSAL_SCHEDULE.md.

Subscription expiry alone does not delete customer data. Under the intended commercial model, the workspace downgrades to Basic and remains accessible.
