# Founder Decisions — Ownership, Privacy and Brand

**Decision date:** 2026-11-26  
**Authority:** Founder  
**Repository:** `Leano-Jordan/ZazuEMP`  
**Canonical write target:** `main` only  
**Status:** Current authoritative Founder decision record; supersedes conflicting assumptions in older working drafts.  
**Purpose:** Preserve decisions so the Founder is not repeatedly asked to restate them.

## 1. Current ownership and future company

- Zazu EMP and its current intellectual property are owned by the Founder personally, until further notice.
- Rosscore Labs is not registered yet. It is the intended future business identity and intended future owner/operator, but it is not currently an incorporated legal entity and does not currently own Zazu.
- No transfer, assignment or backdated company ownership is authorised or implied.
- After Rosscore Labs is properly established, the Founder will decide and document the appropriate IP transfer/assignment and operating arrangement.
- The Founder currently works alone. There are no employees, co-founders, contractors or other people operating Zazu.
- For present-day legal/commercial records, the Founder is the individual solo operator. Do not describe Rosscore Labs as the current contracting party.

## 2. Product stage and launch posture

- Zazu is not launching commercially now. Product stability and engineering acceptance come first; launch is the final stage.
- A controlled, non-commercial pilot with close, trusted members is acceptable when the Founder authorises it.
- This is not public beta, general availability, a paid pilot or permission to advertise, collect subscriptions or make public service promises.
- Until the pilot data approach and privacy controls are confirmed, prefer synthetic/test data. If real personal information is used, applicable privacy duties still apply even in a private test.
- Do not let legal/brand paperwork distract from product stability, but maintain the records and close mandatory obligations before processing real customer data or commercial launch.

## 3. Personal information scope

The Founder confirms the intended V1 information scope includes:
- business/workspace and staff account details;
- customer names and contact details;
- event addresses, dates, requirements and operational notes;
- quotes, invoices and payment records;
- business documents, receipts, photographs and attachments.

The product is not intended to collect identity-document numbers, bank-account details, children's information, medical records or other sensitive/special-category information as a V1 feature. This is a product-scope statement, not a guarantee that free-text notes or uploaded files could never contain such information. UI guidance, data minimisation and support procedures must discourage unnecessary sensitive information.

## 4. Deployment and third-party services

- The application has not yet had production hosting, cloud storage, analytics, payment processing, messaging, monitoring or external support services provisioned/configured for Zazu.
- No third-party provider is currently approved as an engaged Zazu processor/sub-operator merely because a provider was previously shortlisted or an integration is technically possible.
- Hosted and local/offline deployment remain the intended product direction from the existing commercial operating profile. The actual first deployment architecture and providers must be confirmed before personal information is processed in a hosted environment.
- xneelo remains a historical/research shortlist only; it is not selected, provisioned or contracted.
- The existing owner contact mailbox `rosscorelabs@outlook.com` is a provisional human contact, not evidence that an email-delivery integration or Zazu hosting service is configured.

## 5. Information Officer and privacy contact

- The Founder has not registered an Information Officer with the Information Regulator and has not previously been given a practical explanation of the role.
- The Founder is the only person involved and is the proposed Information Officer for the current individual/solo operation, subject to confirming the correct registration route and current legal status with the Information Regulator.
- No Deputy Information Officer is currently appointed; the one-person operation has no additional staff.
- An Information Officer oversees the body's POPIA/PAIA compliance, handles or oversees privacy/access requests, and works with the Regulator. Registration is a formal step; the Founder must not claim to be registered until evidence exists.
- The Founder approves the recommendation to establish a dedicated privacy contact channel before release. The actual mailbox/domain is not yet created or confirmed. Until then, `rosscorelabs@outlook.com` remains the provisional contact already recorded in the operating documents; test monitoring before publication.
- No Information Officer registration, privacy mailbox, privacy portal or public legal notice is claimed as completed.

## 6. Retention proposal requested by Founder

The Founder asked the Director to propose a practical retention schedule. The existing `RELEASE_RETENTION_DISPOSAL_SCHEDULE.md` is the working recommendation, not yet an approved final policy:
- account/profile and workspace administration data: proposed deletion or de-identification within 90 days after closure, following a 30-day export/retrieval window, unless lawful retention, dispute, investigation or legal hold applies;
- event/customer operational records: customer-controlled retention while needed for business and legal duties; no blanket seven-year rule for all records;
- tax/accounting records: preserve for the applicable customer-specific statutory period; the commonly cited five-year tax and seven-year Companies Act record periods must be applied only to records and circumstances to which they actually apply;
- support/security logs: proposed 24-month baseline, reviewed for necessity and minimised;
- backups: proposed rolling 30-day retention, subject to the actual backup architecture and legal holds;
- attachments/media: retain only while needed, then remove from active/private storage and allow backup copies to expire under the approved schedule.

These are proposals for legal/accounting review and technical feasibility checks, not a Founder-approved statutory interpretation or an implemented deletion guarantee. The product must not promise automated deletion until it is implemented and tested.

## 7. Brand and intellectual property

- The Founder personally owns the current Zazu software IP and is the intended owner of the ZAZU brand/trade-mark rights for now.
- The name **ZAZU is NOT approved as the final product name**. The Founder has not decided whether to retain it or rename.
- The descriptor **Event & Business Operations** and working promise **Run every job. Control every cost. Keep working.** remain acceptable working positioning, not a final public launch approval.
- The Founder intends to protect/sell in South Africa with international expansion in mind. This is strategic intent, not a completed multi-country clearance or filing instruction.
- No ZAZU domain, social account, logo registration or trade-mark application has been registered/secured by the Founder to date.
- The Founder is prioritising a stable, worthwhile product over spending money on name registration now. No filing, paid search, legal engagement or expensive brand commitment is authorised by this decision record.
- Existing collision evidence means the name remains a HIGH-risk unresolved choice. Do not represent ZAZU as cleared, registered or safe to use. Preserve a fallback naming option until a later Founder decision.
