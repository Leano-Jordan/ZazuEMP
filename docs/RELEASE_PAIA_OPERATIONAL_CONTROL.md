## Purpose

Provide a controlled process for requests for records held by the relevant private body where PAIA applies.

## Applicability

The responsible party must determine:

- whether PAIA applies to the entity/deployment;
- what records are covered;
- the applicable access procedure;
- who is authorised to make decisions;
- what manual/automated records are maintained;
- what exclusions or refusal grounds may apply.

This repository does not make that legal determination.

## Record inventory

Zazu may hold records such as:

- business/workspace records;
- customer/contact records;
- events/jobs and operational notes;
- quotes, invoices and payment records;
- documents and attachments;
- audit/security records;
- account and authentication records.

The final PAIA process must not disclose another person's protected personal information merely because a record contains it.

## Request handling

1. Receive the request through the approved channel.
2. Record the request identifier and date.
3. Verify the requester and authority where appropriate.
4. Identify responsive records and the business/context concerned.
5. Assess applicable disclosure restrictions and exemptions.
6. Obtain the required approval.
7. Respond using the legally applicable form/timing/process.
8. Record the outcome and any appeal/escalation route.

## Technical boundary

Zazu's business isolation and role-based access controls are technical safeguards for normal application use. A PAIA request is a separate records-access process and must not be implemented by simply giving a requester ordinary application access.
