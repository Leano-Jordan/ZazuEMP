## 2. Copyright

South African copyright protection covers eligible original works, including computer programs. CIPC states that most eligible copyright works do not require registration and that protection is secured automatically when the legal requirements are met.

The Copyright Act provides a general author-ownership rule, subject to statutory exceptions and agreements. Employment, commissioned works, joint authorship and third-party contributions therefore require separate factual review.

Zazu action:
- Keep Isaac identified as current owner of original solo-developed project material.
- Keep proprietary copyright notices in the repository.
- Preserve Git history and dated artifacts.
- Do not claim ownership over third-party components.
- Obtain written contributor/contractor terms before accepting material where ownership matters.

Zazu, Zazu EMP, logos, slogans and distinctive visual identity are separate from copyright.

CIPC states that registered trade marks receive statutory protection under the Trade Marks Act, while unregistered marks may have common-law protection.

Before substantial commercial investment in the brand:

## 6. Personal information and POPIA

Zazu processes or is designed to process names, contact details and identifiable photos. Before real production use, the actual responsible-party/operator arrangement must be documented.

Engineering gates:
- authentication and session security;
- business membership and server-side data isolation;
- roles and permissions;
- controlled media access;
- purpose limitation and data minimisation;
- retention/deletion rules;
- incident response;
- operator agreements where third parties process information;
- backup and recovery controls;
- data-subject access/correction/deletion handling where applicable.

POPIA section 19 requires appropriate, reasonable technical and organisational measures and regular verification and updating of safeguards. Section 21 addresses written contracts with operators that process personal information on behalf of a responsible party. Section 22 addresses security-compromise notification.
