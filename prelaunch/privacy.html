PRIVACY & ENQUIRIES

Privacy notice

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This notice explains how information submitted through the Zazu EMP pre-launch page is handled. Zazu EMP is in development. This page is a marketing and enquiry channel, not a user account or production application.

Operational note: enquiry forms are routed through FormSubmit, a third-party form delivery service, to rosscorelabs@outlook.com. Before promoting this page, the site owner must verify the recipient address with the service and confirm that submissions are delivered as expected.

Information you may provide

The form may collect your name, business name, email address, optional phone/WhatsApp number, business type, your selected interest (early access, demo or both), and any workflow challenge you choose to describe. Do not submit customer lists, financial account details, identity documents, passwords or other sensitive information.

Why we use it

We use the information you submit to respond to your enquiry, assess whether an early-access or demonstration conversation is appropriate, and understand business workflow needs. We do not intend to publish enquiry details or sell them as a contact list.

Service providers and transfers

Form submissions are processed by FormSubmit and delivered by email. That means information is handled by a third-party service and may be processed in infrastructure outside South Africa. Review the provider’s own privacy terms before submitting. GitHub Pages hosts this static website; GitHub may process technical request data under its own terms.

Retention and security

Enquiry information may remain in the recipient email account and the delivery provider’s systems according to their retention practices. Access to the recipient mailbox should be restricted and protected with multi-factor authentication. Do not use this form to send confidential business information. This static page does not create a Zazu account.

Your choices and requests

You can choose not to submit the form. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of enquiry information held in the recipient mailbox, contact rosscorelabs@outlook.com and describe the request. We will respond subject to applicable law and any records we are legally required to retain.

Cookies and analytics

This page does not intentionally load third-party analytics or advertising trackers. GitHub Pages and your browser may process technical data needed to deliver the website.

Contact

For privacy questions about pre-launch enquiries, email rosscorelabs@outlook.com.
