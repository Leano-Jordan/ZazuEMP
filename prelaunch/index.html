GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you sign up.

Is Zazu EMP available now?

Zazu is in active development. Use this form to request early access or a demo; we’ll confirm what is available and the next steps directly.

What kinds of event businesses is Zazu for?

Zazu is being developed for a broad mix of event-related work: equipment and furniture hire, DJs and sound, event planning, décor, photography, food services, baking, funeral services and businesses that combine services. The aim is to connect the operational work behind the job, not to serve only one trade.

Will Zazu work offline?

Offline capability is part of the product direction. Exact availability and limits will depend on the workflows and release version; ask for a demo to discuss your needs.

How much will it cost?

Pricing has not been published on this pre-launch page. Request a demo and we’ll share relevant information when it is available.
