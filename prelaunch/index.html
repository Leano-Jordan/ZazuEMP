OPERATIONAL FOUNDATIONS

Built for the realities of event work.

Field conditions, business privacy and financial accuracy belong in the operating model—not as badges added after the fact.

01 / FIELD

Mobile & offline workflows

Designed around venue, crew and event-day hand-offs. Offline actions and sync behaviour are checked workflow by workflow before each capability is presented as ready.

02 / CONTROL

Access, privacy & POPIA

Business-scoped access, role permissions, retention and recovery are release controls. South African POPIA obligations shape the production-readiness work.

03 / ECOSYSTEM

Connected business workflows

Integration priorities include accounting tools such as Xero and Sage, WhatsApp Business coordination, and Yoco, PayFast and EFT payment tracking. Provider support is listed only after implementation and verification.

GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you sign up.

What does Zazu bring together?

Enquiries, customer requirements, quotations, event/job planning, preparation, scheduling and job-cost visibility. The walkthrough shows the currently available workflows.

Who is Zazu designed for?

Event and catering businesses across equipment/furniture hire, DJs and sound, planning, décor, photography, food and baking, funeral services, and operators combining several services.

What about offline work?

Offline support is a core product direction. Supported actions and sync behaviour are validated at workflow level; the walkthrough can clarify the current limits for your use case.

Which integrations are available?

Accounting, messaging and payment workflows are integration priorities, including Xero, Sage, WhatsApp Business, Yoco, PayFast and EFT tracking. A provider is described as supported only after its connection is implemented and verified.

How are privacy and POPIA handled?

POPIA obligations, business access boundaries, retention and recovery are part of production readiness. Product-specific privacy and hosting details will be documented before customer business data is onboarded. See the privacy notice for pre-launch enquiries.

How much will it cost?

Pricing is not published yet. Request a walkthrough for current availability and relevant commercial information.
