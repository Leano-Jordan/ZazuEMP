## South African copyright position

South African copyright law protects eligible original works, including computer programs, subject to the requirements and exceptions in the Copyright Act 98 of 1978.

CIPC states that copyright in most eligible works does not require registration and is secured automatically when the requirements are met. The Copyright Act states as a general rule that copyright vests in the author, subject to section 21 exceptions and agreements.

Because Zazu EMP is presently recorded as Isaac's own solo work, this project record treats Isaac as the current owner of his original Zazu EMP work, subject to the actual facts and applicable law.

This record must not be read as confirming ownership of third-party code, commissioned work, jointly authored work, employee-created work or material supplied by another person.
